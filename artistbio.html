Billie Wynn is a New York City-based artist and recently graduated from the Rhode Island School of Design’s Painting program in June 2025. Her work explores the relationship between observation, memory, and the ways people absorb and influence one another. She is drawn to faces, figures, and moments of connection, especially the feeling of eye contact and the exchange that happens between people. Billie co-runs Should We Gallery, a collaborative exhibition space committed to experimentation, dialogue, and community care. As both an artist and curator, she sees her work as a form of relationship-building—creating spaces, images, and moments that explore the tension between intimacy and distance.
